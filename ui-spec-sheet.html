Notes · unless otherwise specified

  1. Do not scale from screen; use coded dimensions and register.
  2. Measurements are CSS px at 1×. Main render measured at 1800 px viewport; mobile at 390 px.
  3. Slots replace copy, project names, logos and imagery. No content-width dimensions are specified.

Layout conventions

  1. HUG follows content; FLEX takes remaining room; FILL spans its parent.
  2. Solid lines are containers; dashed lines are flexible slots; gray fill marks inset or hug regions.
  3. Responsive breakpoints are source-defined at 780 px and 480 px. Intermediate widths follow the applicable CSS rules.
